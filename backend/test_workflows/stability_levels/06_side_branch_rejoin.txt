Start rejection review.

The operator scans the rejected container.
The system displays the rejection reason.

The supervisor reviews the rejection reason.
If the rejection reason is accepted, the workflow continues to QA verification.

If the rejection reason needs correction, the supervisor enters the corrected rejection reason.
After correction, the workflow returns to QA verification.

QA verifies the rejection details.
If QA approves, the system records the approved rejection.
If QA rejects, the workflow ends as rejection review failed.

End rejection review.
